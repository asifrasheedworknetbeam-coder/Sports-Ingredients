How we work

We do the homework, so your product is right the first time.

Every product goes through the same three steps: research and formulation, production, and quality assurance. One team, one process, from your first idea to the finished pack.

01 · R&D and formulation

A formula that works, tastes right and is allowed on the label.

Our R&D team turns your idea into a recipe: the right actives and doses, taste, solubility and mouthfeel, checked against EU rules for ingredients and claims.

  • Formulation and dosing
  • Taste, texture and solubility testing
  • Regulatory and label check
R&D and formulation
02 · Production and blending

One partner, from blend to finished pack.

Powders are now blended in-house. Capsules, tablets, softgels and gummies are produced to our formula and specifications, under our quality system. Every product is filled, labelled and checked at NutraPack, our IFS Food certified site in Nijkerk.

  • In-house powder blendingNEW
  • Capsules, tablets and softgels
  • Filling and packing in your format, on four filling lines
Production
03 · Quality assurance

Quality is built in, not checked at the end.

We work to IFS standards across the whole chain: approved suppliers, testing by independent laboratories and full documentation with every batch. So the product you put your brand on is safe, consistent and ready for retail.
