Questions brands ask us

What is private label sports nutrition?+

Products made by a specialised manufacturer and sold under your own brand. You decide on the formula, the format and the pack. We take care of development, production, filling and quality.

What is the minimum order?+

It depends on the format. The table above shows the minimum quantity and packaging per product and flavour for each format. Not sure what fits your plans? Ask us, we think along.

Can I use my own formula?+

Yes. Send it to us and our R&D team checks it for taste, solubility, cost and EU rules before production.

Who makes the label design?+

You supply the artwork. We check it against the EU labelling rules before it goes to print.

How fast do I get a quote?+

Within 3 working days for standard formulas. Complex formulas need more time; we tell you upfront when to expect your quote.

Where are the products made?+

Powders are blended in-house in Nijkerk and filled at NutraPack, our IFS Food certified site. The other formats are produced to our formula and specifications, under our quality system.
